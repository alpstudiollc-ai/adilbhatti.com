What should I write down after a halaqah?
The one thing you did not know before, the ayah or hadith it came from, and the question you did not ask out loud. Three lines. Anything longer does not survive contact with a real week. 200 pages of room in this one.

How do I keep my Muslim friendships strong as we all get busier?
The pattern that holds is low effort and high frequency rather than the reverse. Most groups die waiting for someone to organise something big. Keeping a written list of who you have not spoken to sounds clinical and works better than intending to remember.
How do I make du'a for my friends consistently?
Write the names down. Intentions to remember people evaporate; a list on a page does not. Keeping it in the same book with Bismillah at the top of every page is a small reinforcement of what the list is for.
What is a good way to stay connected with Muslim friends who live far away?
Shared rituals beat scheduled calls, because calls get rescheduled and rituals do not. Reading the same thing, tracking the same goal, writing in the same notebook and comparing. That last one is why this notebook is bought in multiples.
How do I find a good Muslim friend group?
The honest answer is proximity and repetition rather than searching. Halaqah, class, the same masjid on the same night. People become your people by being in the same room repeatedly. Writing down who you keep running into is a strange but effective way to notice it happening.
What is the difference between Hijabi Gang and Hijabi Queen?
