The Age of Disclosure: Is There a Book?

Adil Bhatti · Updated 26 October 2026

Short answerNo. The Age of Disclosure, Dan Farah's documentary, premiered at South by Southwest on March 9, 2025 and reached Prime Video on November 21, 2025, and there is no companion book, official or otherwise. People search for one because the film is 34 interviews with almost no documents on screen, and viewers come out wanting to read what those people have put in writing. Five books do that job. Four are by or about the people in the film. The fifth, Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution, is the one that puts their testimony at the end of a record that starts three thousand years earlier.

What the film is, and what it is not

The Age of Disclosure is built entirely from interviews: 34 of them, including Luis Elizondo, the former head of the Pentagon's AATIP program, Jay Stratton, the former director of the UAP Task Force, Senators Marco Rubio and Kirsten Gillibrand, and James Clapper, the former Director of National Intelligence. Its claim is that the United States has been running a secret program around recovered non human craft for eighty years and that disclosure is now under way.

What it is not is a documentary of documents. Almost nothing is shown on screen that a viewer can check. That is why the search for a book exists: people want to read the sourced version of what they just heard. There is no book of the film. There are books by the people in it, and there is one book that puts the whole thing in order.

Five books that document what the film says

  • Imminent, Luis Elizondo, 2024. The film's central voice, in his own words, at book length. AATIP, the Pentagon, and what he says he saw. A bestseller, and the closest thing to the film in print.
  • In Plain Sight, Ross Coulthart, 2021. The Australian journalist's investigation that preceded and fed much of the film's framing. Sourced, skeptical in method, startling in conclusion.
  • UFO: The Inside Story of the US Government's Search for Alien Life Here and Out There, Garrett Graff, 2023. The mainstream history. Where the government programs in the film came from, from 1947 to the 2023 hearings, written by a journalist with no stake in the answer.
  • American Cosmic, Diana Walsh Pasulka, 2019. A religion scholar's account of the scientists and insiders who believe, and why. The film's believers, explained from outside.
  • Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution, Adil Bhatti, 2025. What people get wrong

Searching for a companion book
There is none. Amazon listings using the title are unrelated or unofficial. Buy the books by the people in the film instead.
Confusing it with Disclosure Day
Different film. Disclosure Day is Spielberg's fiction, released June 12, 2026. The Age of Disclosure is a documentary. Our guide to the Spielberg film is separate.
Treating the interviews as documents
They are testimony. The documents are in the books, and increasingly at war.gov/ufo.
Starting with the ancient material
Start with the people you just watched: Imminent or Graff. Come to the ancient record when you want to know where it all began.

Questions and answers

Is there a book of The Age of Disclosure?
No. Dan Farah's documentary has no companion book, official or otherwise. The closest things in print are Imminent by Luis Elizondo, who is the film's central voice, and Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs, which puts the film's witnesses at the end of the full record.
When did The Age of Disclosure come out?
It premiered at South by Southwest in Austin on March 9, 2025, had a limited theatrical run from November 21, 2025, and began streaming on Prime Video the same day.
Who is in The Age of Disclosure?
34 interviewees, including Luis Elizondo, Jay Stratton, Senators Marco Rubio and Kirsten Gillibrand, and former Director of National Intelligence James Clapper, alongside scientists, military officers, and former intelligence officials.
What should I read after watching The Age of Disclosure?
Imminent for Elizondo's own account, In Plain Sight for the sourced investigation, Graff's UFO for the history of the programs, and Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs for the whole arc from the ancient record to the 2023 hearings.
Is The Age of Disclosure the same as Disclosure Day?
No. The Age of Disclosure is a documentary from 2025. Disclosure Day is Steven Spielberg's fictional film from June 2026. People confuse them constantly.
Does Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs cover the people in The Age of Disclosure?
Its final part covers Luis Elizondo and the exposure of AATIP, the Pentagon's confirmed Navy footage, and the 2023 Congressional hearings with David Grusch, Ryan Graves, and Commander David Fravor. The three parts before that are the record those witnesses stand on.
Is The Age of Disclosure credible?
Its interviewees are real and senior. Its central claim, an eighty year secret program around recovered craft, rests on their testimony rather than on documents shown on screen. Reviewers split on exactly that point. The books on this list are where the sourcing lives.
Where can I watch The Age of Disclosure?
Prime Video, worldwide, since November 21, 2025.
What is the Pentagon doing about UFO disclosure now?
Publishing declassified UAP records on a rolling basis at war.gov/ufo under the PURSUE program, which began May 8, 2026, after the film was finished. Our PURSUE guide explains how to read them.
Which book covers both the ancient evidence and the modern witnesses?
Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution. The Sumerian tablets and the Nazca Lines at one end, the 2023 hearings at the other, and the documented cases in between.
