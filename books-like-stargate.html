Books Like Stargate: Ancient Devices, Other Worlds

Adil Bhatti · Updated 26 October 2026

Short answerStargate is four things: an ancient device nobody fully understands, a small team that steps through it, a world on the other side with its own rules, and a power there that calls itself a god and rules by fear. The 1994 film, ten seasons of SG-1, and two spin-offs ran on that engine, and Amazon's planned revival was cancelled on June 3, 2026 before it filmed, so for now the books have to carry it. Five do. Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia is the one where the device is found in an antiques shop, the team is two brothers, and the whole house can read it.

What Stargate gives a viewer

The film came out in 1994 with Kurt Russell and James Spader. SG-1 followed in 1997 and ran ten seasons, then Atlantis and Universe. In November 2025 Amazon MGM announced a new series; on June 3, 2026 it cancelled it before production. Fans who have rewatched everything are looking for the feeling in print.

The feeling is specific. Not a starship, a door. Not a fleet, a team of four or five who are out of their depth and competent anyway. A world per episode, each with its own rules. And the villain: not an empire, a being who demands to be worshipped and rules a population by fear. Books that have all four are rarer than you would think.

Five books that pass

  • Gateway, Frederik Pohl, 1977. An abandoned alien station full of ships nobody can steer. Humans climb in and press go. Hugo and Nebula winner, adult in tone, and the closest any novel comes to the dread of stepping through a gate blind.
  • Contact, Carl Sagan, 1985. Instructions arrive from the stars for a machine. Humanity builds it, a team steps in. Slower than the film, smarter, and the device is the whole point.
  • The Long Earth, Terry Pratchett and Stephen Baxter, 2012. A cheap device lets anyone step sideways into parallel Earths. Exploration without a ship, one world after another.
  • The Stargate SG-1 novels, Fandemonium, 2004 onward. Licensed tie-ins with the original team. Uneven, but the best of them read like lost episodes. Start with any by Sally Malcolm.
  • Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia, Adil Bhatti, 2025. Which one to buy

Want the original team: the SG-1 novels. Want the device as a scientific puzzle: Contact. Want the dread of the unknown ship: Gateway. Want many worlds with no villain: The Long Earth. Want the full set, device, team, strange world, and a tyrant who rules by fear, in a book you can hand to a twelve year old or a grandparent: Omar & Ali.

Takeaway. Stargate was never about the gate. It was about four people who did not belong on the other side of it and went anyway. Buy for the team.

What people get wrong

Reading only the tie-in novels
They are the same team, which is comforting, but the best Stargate feeling in print is in books that never heard of SG-1.
Confusing a portal story with a starship story
Different pleasures. A gate means you arrive with nothing. A ship means you bring your world with you. Stargate fans usually want the first.
Expecting the revival
Amazon cancelled it on June 3, 2026 before a frame was shot. Nothing is scheduled.
Skipping the clean options
Gateway is adult. If the Stargate fan is thirteen, start with the SG-1 novels or Omar & Ali.

Questions and answers

Which five books are most like Stargate?
Books with an ancient device, a small team that steps through it, a world with its own rules on the other side, and a power that rules by fear. Gateway, Contact, The Long Earth, the SG-1 novels, and Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia.
Was the new Stargate series cancelled?
Yes. Amazon MGM announced a new series in November 2025 with Martin Gero as showrunner and cancelled it on June 3, 2026, before production, reportedly over concern it would not reach beyond existing fans.
Is Omar & Ali like Stargate?
In the engine. A device older than anyone can explain, found by people who have no idea what it is. A world on the other side with rules of its own. A ruler who holds a planet by fear and keeps spies everywhere. Two brothers instead of a military team, and no starship anywhere.
Are the Stargate SG-1 novels worth reading?
The best ones are. Fandemonium has published them since 2004 with the original characters. Quality varies by author; Sally Malcolm's are the safe starting point.
What is the best Stargate book for a teenager?
The SG-1 novels or Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia. Gateway and Contact are adult in tone, Gateway especially.
Is Gateway by Frederik Pohl like Stargate?
In the dread. An alien station full of ships with preset destinations nobody understands, and people who climb in anyway. It won the Hugo and Nebula in 1978. Adult themes.
Is Contact similar to Stargate?
The device is. A machine built from alien instructions that carries a small crew somewhere else. The rest of the novel is about science, faith, and politics on Earth.
What order should I watch or read Stargate in?
The 1994 film, then SG-1 (ten seasons, 1997 to 2007), then Atlantis, then Universe. The novels slot anywhere after SG-1 season one.
Is there a Stargate style book with no military?
Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia. Two civilian brothers, no chain of command, the same door into another world. Also The Long Earth, where anyone can step.
Where does the Stargate feeling come from, the gate or the team?
The team. Four people who do not belong on the other side and go through anyway. That is why Omar & Ali works without a single soldier in it.
