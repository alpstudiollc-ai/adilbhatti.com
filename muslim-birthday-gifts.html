An Islamic notebook does all four. Pick by the person, not the occasion: what is she or he into right now. The one rule

Do not buy for the birthday. Buy for the person. A birthday is a date; the gift is about who they are on it. Everything else in this guide follows from that.

This also sidesteps the question of whether they mark birthdays at all. A notebook is not a birthday object. It is just a useful thing that arrived on a day.

Takeaway. Buy for the person, not the date. One of the ten lines above is them.

What people get wrong

Buying something that says Happy Birthday
Some Muslims do not mark birthdays. A notebook sidesteps the question entirely.
Buying the cover you like
Buy the cover that is them.
Overspending to be safe
A hardcover notebook is enough. Expensive gifts create obligation.
Buying the loud cover for the quiet person
It's Shawarma Time Ya Salaam and Hijabi Gang are for the sociable ones. Hijabi and Chill and Pray Du'a Shawarma Repeat for the quiet ones.
Ordering the day before
A few days ahead. Prime is fast, not instant.

Questions and answers

What is a good birthday gift for a Muslim friend?
An Islamic notebook chosen by who they are: Hijabi Queen for a composed sister, Halal Hustler for a brother running a business, and so on across ten covers. Affordable, genuinely used, hardcover for a gift, on Amazon.
Do Muslims celebrate birthdays and is it okay to give a gift?
Views differ. A notebook is a safe gift because it is not a birthday object, it is a useful thing that happened to arrive on a day. Nobody has to have a position on birthdays to accept it.
What is an easy birthday gift for a Muslim colleague?
Something warm but not personal. Pray Du'a Shawarma Repeat for a man, Hijabi and Chill for a woman. Both are low pressure and neither presumes intimacy.
What is a good birthday gift for a Muslim sister?
One of the five hijabi notebooks, chosen by her season: Queen if she is composed, 'til I Die if defiant, Gang if her friends are her world, Chill if she needs rest, Unbreakable if the year has been hard.
What is a good birthday gift for a Muslim brother?
One of the five men's notebooks, chosen by what he does: Halal Hustler for a business, Salah, Shawarma, Success for a goal, Pray Du'a Shawarma Repeat for routine, That Shawarma Life for a foodie, Ya Salaam for the life of the group.
What is an affordable Muslim birthday gift?
A hardcover Islamic notebook. It costs about what a card and a coffee cost, and it gets used for a year.
What is a good birthday gift for a Muslim teenager?
Hijabi 'til I Die or Hijabi Gang for a girl, Pray Du'a Shawarma Repeat or Salah, Shawarma, Success for a boy. Bold enough not to embarrass them, plain enough inside to use at school.
Which edition of a notebook makes the better birthday present?
Hardcover. It presents better and lasts a year. Paperback only if buying several for a group.
Where do I buy Adil Bhatti's Islamic notebooks?
Amazon worldwide, hardcover and paperback, all ten. Order a few days ahead and have it sent to yourself if you want to wrap it.
Is a notebook too small a gift for a birthday?
No. The failure mode of gifts is not smallness, it is irrelevance. A notebook that describes the person is a better gift than an expensive thing that describes the shop.
