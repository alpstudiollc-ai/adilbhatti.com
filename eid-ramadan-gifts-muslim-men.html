The occasions, and what each one wants

Eid al-Fitr. He has fasted thirty days and has strong feelings about the first proper meal. It's Shawarma Time Ya Salaam gets a laugh at the table and then gets used. Hardcover for a gift.

Eid al-Adha. The family gathering. Something that works in a room. Pray Du'a Shawarma Repeat, deadpan, or Ya Salaam, loud.

Ramadan. Give it before the month. Founders and goal setters reset in Ramadan and want paper. Halal Hustler for the business, Salah, Shawarma, Success for everything else he is chasing.

New business or promotion. Halal Hustler for a shop, a startup, a freelance career. Salah, Shawarma, Success for a promotion or a degree.

Graduation. Salah, Shawarma, Success. It is about what comes next.

He eats shawarma more than is reasonable. That Shawarma Life. He has been seen.

Why a notebook gets past the nothing

Because it does not ask anything of him. It is not a subscription, it is not a course, and it does not sit on a shelf accusing him. It is 200 college ruled pages with Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Raheem at the top of every one, and a cover that makes a true joke about his week. He can fill it or lose it in a drawer. Either way it cost less than a dinner out.

Takeaway. He says he wants nothing. Give him something that is about what he does, and he will use it.

What people get wrong

Buying a serious gift for a funny man
Read the cover. The whole point of this collection is that it is funny outside and plain inside.
Buying Halal Hustler for a man with no business
It is specifically for founders and self employed brothers. Salah, Shawarma, Success is the general ambition one.
Giving It's Shawarma Time Ya Salaam in a hard year
It is the loudest cover. Save it for good news.
Buying paperback as a gift
Hardcover for gifts. Paperback is for his own spares.
Ordering on Eid morning
Order a week ahead. Eid moves with the moon.
