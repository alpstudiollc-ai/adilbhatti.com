Books Like Percy Jackson: Hidden Worlds, Fast Chapters

Adil Bhatti · Updated 26 October 2026

Short answerLike Percy Jackson means four things: an ordinary kid who turns out to belong to a hidden world, friends who become a team, a villain with real power, and chapters short enough that nobody notices they have read two hundred pages. Season two of the Disney+ series arrived in December 2025 and sent a new wave of readers through the books in a week. Six books carry the same four ingredients. What Percy Jackson gives a reader

Rick Riordan published The Lightning Thief in 2005. Twenty years, five series, and a Disney+ show later, the formula is still the most reliable thing in children's publishing. A kid who does not fit in. A discovery that explains why. A world hiding behind the ordinary one. A quest with friends. A villain who is not a joke. And a narrator who is funny about all of it.

Readers who finish the series are not asking for more Greek gods. They are asking for that feeling: the door opening, the friends, the pace. These six deliver it in six different hidden worlds.

Six books that pass

  • The Lightning Thief, Rick Riordan, 2005. If the reader came from the show and has not read the book, start here. The book is faster and funnier than the show, and Percy is twelve in it.
  • Keeper of the Lost Cities, Shannon Messenger, 2012. A girl who reads minds learns she is not human. A hidden elf world, a school, a conspiracy, and nine thick books for the reader who wants to stay a long time.
  • Artemis Fowl, Eoin Colfer, 2001. A twelve year old criminal mastermind kidnaps a fairy. The hidden world is underground and technological. Sharper and colder than Percy, very funny.
  • Aru Shah and the End of Time, Roshani Chokshi, 2018. From Riordan's own imprint. Hindu mythology, a girl who lights a lamp she should not have, and a sidekick who steals the book.
  • Amari and the Night Brothers, B.B. Alston, 2021. A girl from the projects gets a scholarship to a secret bureau of supernatural affairs. The hidden world as a summer camp with stakes.
  • Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia, Adil Bhatti, 2025. Which one to buy

Want more of exactly Percy: Aru Shah or Amari, both built on his blueprint. Want a world the reader can live in for a year: Keeper of the Lost Cities. Want something sharper: Artemis Fowl. Want the same ride with spaceships instead of gods, and a book the parents will read too: Omar & Ali.

Age is not the issue with any of these. Percy readers run from nine to forty. Pick by what kind of hidden world they want next.

Takeaway. Nobody loves Percy Jackson for the mythology. They love the kid, the friends, and the pace. Buy for those three and the setting can be anything, including another planet.

What people get wrong

Buying more Greek mythology
Riordan's other series are good, but the reader wanted the feeling, not the pantheon. Any hidden world works.
Assuming it has to be fantasy
Science fiction does the same job. A portal to a planet is a door to Olympus with better vehicles.
Picking by age label
Percy Jackson is shelved for ten year olds and read by adults. So is everything on this list.
Buying the longest series first
Keeper of the Lost Cities is nine thick books. Start a reluctant reader on something that ends.

Questions and answers

Which books are most like Percy Jackson?
Books with the same four ingredients: an ordinary kid, a hidden world, friends who become a team, and short chapters. Aru Shah and Amari and the Night Brothers are the closest. Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia is the one with an alien planet instead of Olympus.
Are there books like Percy Jackson but science fiction?
Yes. Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia: two brothers from New Jersey, a device that opens a portal, and a planet with its own rules and its own tyrant. Same door opening feeling, with an alien world behind it instead of a mythological one.
Is Omar & Ali like Percy Jackson?
In the shape. Ordinary kids from the New York area, a discovery that pulls them into a hidden world, friends made along the way, a villain who is frightening rather than funny, and chapters that move. Different in setting: a planet, not a pantheon.
What is the Percy Jackson reading order?
Percy Jackson and the Olympians, five books starting with The Lightning Thief (2005). Then The Heroes of Olympus, then The Trials of Apollo. The Disney+ series follows the first five, with season two covering The Sea of Monsters.
When did Percy Jackson season 2 come out?
December 2025 on Disney+, adapting The Sea of Monsters, the second book. A third season has been ordered.
What is a good book like Percy Jackson for a nine year old?
Aru Shah and the End of Time or Amari and the Night Brothers, both written for the same age. Omar & Ali reads a little older in its threat level, in the register of Star Wars, and suits most ten year olds and up.
Are Rick Riordan Presents books as good as Percy Jackson?
Several are. Aru Shah by Roshani Chokshi is the standout. They apply Riordan's shape to other mythologies, Hindu, Mayan, Korean, Navajo, with Riordan editing.
Is Keeper of the Lost Cities like Percy Jackson?
In the hidden world and the school. It is slower, longer, and more about relationships and conspiracy than quests. Nine books. For the reader who wants to stay, not sprint.
Is Artemis Fowl a good next read after Percy?
For a reader who liked Percy's attitude more than his heart. Artemis is the villain of his own book at first. Very funny, slightly cold, eight books.
Which book like Percy Jackson can the whole family read?
Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia. Adults and teenagers read it, there is no profanity, and the brothers' relationship is written for anyone who has one.
