Questions about the guides

What are the guides on adilbhatti.com?
Short, plain English articles on subjects the books and notebooks cover. Some explain a case or a genre, some help a reader or a gift buyer choose. Each one ends by pointing to the product it serves. New ones are added roughly weekly and listed here newest first.
Which guide should I read first if I care about the Pentagon UFO files?
Start with UAP vs UFO for the vocabulary, then How to Read the Pentagon's PURSUE UAP Files, then the 1942 Battle of Los Angeles guide for why the record starts there, then Ancient Astronaut Theory for the other end of the record, then Best UFO Books to Read in 2026.
Which guide should I read if I just watched Disclosure Day?
What to Read After Disclosure Day. It separates what in the film is real from what is invented and points to the documented record.
Which guide should I read if I am buying a book for a Muslim reader?
Sci-Fi Books with Muslim Heroes for the checklist, Sci-Fi Adventure Books for Reluctant Readers if they do not read much, Books Like Star Wars if they want epic war and rebellion, Books Like Star Trek if they want a crew and friendships, and Books Like Dune if they loved the films and want the desert and the empire without the density.
Which guide should I read if I am buying an Islamic gift?
Eid and Ramadan Gifts for Muslim Women or for Muslim Men, by occasion, Umrah and Hajj Gifts if someone is going on pilgrimage or has just come home, or Easy Muslim Birthday Gifts if it is a birthday and you do not want to overthink it.
Do the guides cover the notebooks?
Yes, five of them: the Eid and Ramadan gift guides for women and for men, the birthday gift guide, the Umrah and Hajj gift guide, and What to Write in an Islamic Journal, which is the method once you own one.
Are the guides about the books or about the subjects?
The subjects. Each guide is written to be useful whether or not you buy anything, and the product is offered at the end for readers who want to go deeper.
How often is a new guide added?
Roughly one a week. The list on this page is newest first, and each guide shows its own updated date at the top.
Are the guides accurate and up to date?
Each guide carries a visible updated date. Guides on moving subjects, such as the Pentagon releases, are revised when the facts change rather than left to go stale.
Can I use these guides for school or a book club?
Yes. Each one has a short answer at the top, a contents list, a comparison table, and a set of questions and answers that stand on their own.
Who writes the guides?
Adil Bhatti, the author of both books and the ten notebooks, publishing under Alpha Leonis Production Studio LLC. No ghostwriter, no content agency.
Where do I find the books and notebooks the guides point to?
Every guide ends with the product it serves and links to its page on this site and to Amazon.
