Questions about the guides

What are the guides on adilbhatti.com?
Short, plain English articles on subjects the books cover. Some explain a case or a genre, some help a reader or a parent choose. Each one ends by pointing to the book it serves. New ones are added roughly weekly and listed here newest first.
Which guide should I read first if I care about the Pentagon UFO files?
Start with How to Read the Pentagon's PURSUE UAP Files. It explains what the program is, what has been released, and how to read a file without misreading it. Then the 1942 Battle of Los Angeles guide for the earliest case in the record.
Which guide should I read if I am buying a book for a Muslim teenager?
Sci-Fi Books with Muslim Heroes: A Parent's Guide. It gives you a four point checklist and an age guide. If you want to understand the genre the book sits in first, read What Is Portal Fantasy.
Do the guides cover the notebooks?
Not yet. The first guides serve the two books. Notebook guides, on choosing an Islamic gift by recipient, are planned.
Are the guides about the books or about the subjects?
The subjects. Each guide is written to be useful whether or not you buy anything, and the book is offered at the end for readers who want to go deeper.
How often is a new guide added?
Roughly one a week. The list on this page is newest first, and each guide shows its own updated date at the top.
Are the guides accurate and up to date?
Each guide carries a visible updated date. Guides on moving subjects, such as the Pentagon releases, are revised when the facts change rather than left to go stale.
Can I use these guides for school or a book club?
Yes. Each one has a short answer at the top, a contents list, a comparison table, and a set of questions and answers that stand on their own. They are written to be quoted.
Who writes the guides?
Adil Bhatti, the author of both books, publishing under Alpha Leonis Production Studio LLC. There is no ghostwriter and no content agency.
Where do I find the books the guides point to?
Every guide ends with the book it serves and a link to its page on this site and to Amazon. The two books are Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia and Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution.
Is there a guide on Disclosure Day?
Not as its own guide yet. The PURSUE guide covers the film's timing and what is and is not real in it, and the Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs book page answers what to read after watching it.
