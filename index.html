About the Author

Adil Bhatti.

Adil Bhatti is an American-Pakistani author whose work crosses continents, cultures, and dimensions. With his home in the United States, he has lived, traveled, and eaten his way through Türkiye, Morocco, Dubai, Japan, England, and far beyond, and that restless curiosity runs through everything he writes. He is a lifelong lover of science fiction, space, and the ancient questions that have followed humanity from the first time we looked up.

His catalog ranges from young adult sci-fi adventure, to investigative non-fiction on UFOs and ancient mysteries, to boldly designed notebooks for readers who want their stationery to say something. At the center of all of it: family, and food worth traveling for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything readers ask, answered.

Who is Adil Bhatti?
An American Pakistani author based in the United States. He writes science fiction, investigative non fiction on UFOs and ancient mysteries, and publishes a collection of ten Islamic notebooks. His debut novel, Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia, was released June 28, 2025. He publishes everything under his own imprint, Alpha Leonis Production Studio LLC.
What books has Adil Bhatti written?
Two full length books. Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia, a portal sci-fi adventure with two Muslim brothers at the centre, released June 2025. And Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution, a non fiction investigation into UFO and UAP disclosure, released December 2025. He also publishes ten Islamic notebooks.
What is Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia about?
Two Moroccan American teen brothers from New Jersey, Omar and Ali, find a triangular alien device in a Tangier antiques shop while on a family holiday. They bring it home, activate it by accident, and a portal opens. On the other side is Orulenthia, a giant alien planet of billions ruled by the sadistic tyrant Lord Thaarn. They land in the middle of a rebellion that includes the alien warrior scholar Vaan, the fierce warrior Nysha, and Vaan's close friend Ytelthuun. 243 pages, published June 28, 2025.
What is Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution about?
A non fiction investigation running from Sumerian accounts of the Anunnaki, Egyptian monuments aligned to the stars, Ezekiel's wheels, and the Nazca Lines, forward through the 1561 Nuremberg event, the 1896 Great Airship Mystery, the 1942 Battle of Los Angeles, and Roswell, and into Rendlesham Forest, the Phoenix Lights, the USS Nimitz Tic Tac encounter, AATIP, and the 2023 Congressional hearings where Ryan Graves, David Grusch, and Commander David Fravor testified under oath. Released December 2025.
Is there a UFO book worth reading while the Pentagon files are coming out?
The Pentagon has been publishing declassified UAP records on a rolling basis since May 2026 under the PURSUE program. Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs was published in December 2025, before the first release, and does not cover those specific files. What it does is map the history they keep landing in, which is what makes an individual file mean anything.
Does Adil Bhatti write fiction or non fiction?
Both, and they are separate books. Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia is fiction, a portal adventure. Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs is non fiction, built on declassified documents and sworn testimony. The interest in what might be out there runs through both, in one case invented and in the other documented.
Which Adil Bhatti book should I read first?
Depends what you want. If you want an adventure, Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia, 243 pages, a weekend. If you want the real record, Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs. They share a preoccupation and nothing else.
Is there a sci-fi novel with Muslim main characters?
Omar & Ali: The Key to Orulenthia is one of very few. Two brothers who find alien technology in an antiques shop and end up on another planet. They are Muslim, and the book does not make that the plot.
What is a good UFO book that covers ancient history too?
Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs: The Disclosure Revolution is built specifically for that. The ancient astronaut literature and the modern disclosure literature almost never talk to each other. This runs both as one line, from Sumerian tablets to Congressional testimony.

Is Omar & Ali suitable for teenagers?
Generally yes. There is no romance and no profanity. Lord Thaarn rules by fear and the book does not soften that, so there is menace in it, roughly in the register of Star Wars. Most parents find it lands from around eleven or twelve upward.
Is the UFO book speculation or documented?
Documented. Every case in Ancient Aliens to Modern UFOs is on the record: the 1942 Battle of Los Angeles, Roswell, Rendlesham Forest, the Phoenix Lights, the USS Nimitz Tic Tac encounter, AATIP, and the 2023 Congressional hearings. It is built from declassified files and sworn testimony. Readers wanting a confident answer about what the objects are will not get one, because the record does not provide it.
