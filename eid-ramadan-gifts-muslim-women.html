Eid and Ramadan Gifts for Muslim Women

Adil Bhatti · Updated 2 October 2026

Short answerMatch the gift to the occasion, not the shop. Eid al-Fitr comes after thirty days of her cooking for everyone, so give rest or recognition. Eid al-Adha is the family gathering, so give something she opens in front of people. Ramadan itself is for resetting, so give it before the month starts. A graduation or new job wants something that goes on a desk. A new baby or a hard year wants something that asks nothing of her. An Islamic notebook with Bismillah on every page fits every one of these, at an affordable price, and the right cover changes with the occasion.

The occasions, and what each one wants

Eid al-Fitr. She has just fed a household for a month. The gift that lands is either rest or recognition. Hijabi and Chill for rest, Hijabi Queen for recognition. Hardcover, because it is a gift.

Eid al-Adha. The bigger gathering. Something she can open at the table. Hijabi Queen or Hijabi 'til I Die, both bold covers that read well in a room.

Ramadan. Give it before the month. Many women reset their goals and du'a in Ramadan and want somewhere to write them. Any of the five works; Hijabi 'til I Die if she is recommitting.

Graduation or new job. Something for the desk. Hijabi Queen in hardcover.

New baby. Everything she receives is for the baby. Give her something that is only hers and asks nothing. Hijabi and Chill.

A hard year. Grief, divorce, illness, a hard job. Nothing celebratory. Hijabi and Unbreakable, quietly.

For a group of friends. One each of Hijabi Gang, Where My Girls At? The title only works in multiples.

Why a notebook works for all of them

Because it is affordable, it is genuinely used, and the cover does the saying so you do not have to find the words. All five hijabi notebooks share the same interior: 200 college ruled pages, Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Raheem in Arabic calligraphy at the top of every one, 6.24 by 9.24 inches. What changes is the cover, and the cover is chosen by who she is right now.

Takeaway. Do not buy for the woman. Buy for the season she is in. The same woman needs a different cover in a hard year than in a good one.

What people get wrong

Giving Hijabi Gang to one person
It is a group notebook. Alone, the title does not land.
Giving a celebratory cover in a hard year
Read the room. Hijabi and Unbreakable exists for exactly this.
Buying for Eid on Eid
Order a week ahead. Amazon Prime helps but it is not instant.
Buying paperback as a gift
Hardcover for gifts. Paperback is for her own second copy.
Giving something religious to a woman who wants rest
Bismillah on the page is enough. Hijabi and Chill does not lecture.
